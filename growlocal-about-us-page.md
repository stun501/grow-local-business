## Notes for the agent

1. **Remove all pricing.** The previous "What it costs" section with the £150 / £450 / £600 ranges is deleted in full and does not appear anywhere on the site. Positioning as a low-cost service works against the premium, trusted-partner feel we want.
2. **The consultation booking is the single conversion goal of this page.** Wire the primary CTA to a booking calendar (Cal.com or Calendly, embedded or linked). If the booking tool isn't set up yet, the button falls back to the text-us action and you flag it.
3. **The example panels must not read as marketing.** If any of them starts to sound like a testimonial or a boast, pull it back toward "a thing that happened to someone you might recognise." Warm, specific, understated.
4. **Where a panel names an industry** (garage, GP practice), keep it — the specificity is what makes it relatable. The examples are illustrative of the type of work; they don't need to be attributed to named clients, and shouldn't invent client names or fake quotes.
5. **Keep it scannable.** This is a longer page than the others. Use the eyebrows and panel labels so someone skimming still gets the whole story.
6. **No "high street," no second brand name, no stock photos, no pricing.** Those are the four things most likely to slip back in.
