For the printer

A5 flyers · ready to print

Two versions. Each file is one front + one back (not a 4-up sheet). Open the version you want, then print or save as PDF and send that PDF to your printer.

Size: A5 portrait with 4mm bleed (page 156 × 218 mm, trim 148 × 210 mm). Stock from the flyer spec: 400gsm uncoated, matte, no lamination.

How to print

  1. Open Arm A or Arm B below in Chrome.
  2. Press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac).
  3. Destination: Save as PDF (or print directly).
  4. Paper; custom 156 × 218 mm · Margins; None · Background graphics; ON.
  5. You should get 2 pages (front, then back). Tell the printer: double-sided A5 with 4mm bleed.
