Where to use it

  1. In your text replies

    The single highest-value placement.

    1. Save the link as a shortcut on your phone.
    2. When someone asks about availability, send the link instead of a time.
    3. Add; "Pick whatever suits and I'll confirm it."

  2. On your Google profile

    Lets people book without ringing.

    1. Paste it into the Appointment link field.
    2. Mention it in a post so it shows in search.

  3. On your voicemail and out-of-hours reply

    Catches people when you're shut.

    1. Change your voicemail; "If it's a booking, text me and I'll send a link."
    2. Set the same as an auto-reply on the business mobile.

Common mistakes

  • Opening the whole diary. Leave yourself gaps. A calendar with no slack is how you end up working late.
  • Slots too long, or too short. Base it on a typical job plus ten minutes, not the best case.
  • No reminder. Most no-shows are forgetfulness, not rudeness. A message the day before catches most of them.

What to expect

This won't fill an empty diary. Nothing does that on its own. What it saves is the arranging, which for most people is a couple of hours a week they were never paid for.
