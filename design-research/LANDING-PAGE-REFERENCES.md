## Five premium examples (Craig’s bar)

### 01 — Apple
- **URL:** https://www.apple.com/  
- **Why premium:** Product is the hero. Almost no chrome. Short headline, two CTAs, perfect photography, one job per section.  
- **Apply to Grow Local:** Instant Quote on a phone as hero. One headline. One teal CTA. Strip card borders.

### 02 — GoodCart
- **URL:** https://www.goodcart.design/  
- **Why premium:** Soft cream stage, bold editorial headline, generous gallery of high-quality screens. Chrome stays quiet so the visuals feel expensive.  
- **Apply to Grow Local:** Three tools as a curated screenshot gallery (not heavy bordered cards). Quiet audience chips.

### 03 — Elicyon
- **URL:** https://www.elicyon.com/  
- **Why premium:** Full-bleed luxury photography, sculptural serif type in the empty space of the image, tiny nav. Mood before features.  
- **Apply to Grow Local:** Flyer: atmosphere + expressive headline + QR. Hub: fewer words over a real visual, not a card stack.

### 04 — Aesop
- **URL:** https://www.aesop.com/  
- **Why premium:** Cinematic dark hero, elegant serif, ghost CTA, sells feeling before product specs.  
- **Apply to Grow Local:** Optional darker/atmospheric hero; one solid teal Scan CTA, secondary actions quiet.

### 05 — Bang & Olufsen
- **URL:** https://www.bang-olufsen.com/  
- **Why premium:** Lifestyle full-bleed, centred product story, UI almost invisible.  
- **Apply to Grow Local:** First screen = brand + one line + one CTA; tools after. Flyer mirrors that restraint.
