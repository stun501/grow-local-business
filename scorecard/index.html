The problem

Your listing is doing more work than your website.

For most local businesses, far more people see the Google listing than ever reach the website. It's what decides whether someone taps "directions" or scrolls to the next result.

And most listings are missing the basics; no photos in two years, no secondary categories set, no hours over Christmas, no replies to reviews, nothing in the description. None of it is difficult. It's just that nobody ever told the owner it mattered.

Where to use it

What to do with your score.

  1. Fix the top three first

    They're ranked by impact for a reason.

    1. Work down the list in order.
    2. Give it twenty minutes in one sitting rather than picking at it.
    3. Re-run the score afterwards to see the change.

  2. Put it in the diary, monthly

    Profiles drift.

    1. Add fifteen minutes to the calendar on the first of the month.
    2. Add a photo, check your hours, reply to any new reviews.

  3. Photos are the quickest win

    Listings with recent photos get noticeably more clicks.

    1. Take five on your phone; the front, inside, your team, your work, the sign.
    2. Upload straight from the Google app.
    3. Add a couple more each month rather than twenty in one go.

Common mistakes

Four things worth avoiding.

Filling it in once and never returning. Google favours listings that show signs of life. A profile untouched for two years slips quietly down.

Leaving categories too narrow. Your primary category is one of the clearest signals to Google about what you actually do, and most businesses never add a second one.

Ignoring the questions section. Anyone can answer a question on your listing, including a competitor. Answer them yourself.

Wrong or missing bank-holiday hours. One of the fastest ways to earn a one-star review from someone who drove over.
