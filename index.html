Common questions

Quick answers before you pick a tool.

Are these tools really free?

Yes. No sign-up wall, no card, and no report emailed to you. You set a tool up and use it. If you ever want help with something bigger, you can text us; if you don't, keep the tools.

Who are these for?

Established local businesses with a real operation; garages, trades, clinics, agents, professional services and similar. If you're purely online, we're probably not the right fit.

How long does a tool take?

Most take about a minute once you have your business name (and prices, if the tool needs them). The Scorecard is usually under thirty seconds.

Will you email me a report?

No. Results stay on the page so you can use them straight away. We don't email tool reports to you.

Who's behind this

Grow Local is run by Craig. I spend most of my week with people who run real businesses; the sort where you're quoting a job in the morning, sorting a supplier at lunch, and doing the invoicing at nine at night because that's when the day finally goes quiet.

What I've noticed is that these businesses rarely lose out because they're not good enough. They lose out because they're busy. The enquiry that came in on a Sunday and never got answered. The customer who'd have left a glowing review if anyone had asked. Small things, easy to miss, and they quietly add up over a year.

So that's what I do; help good local businesses get found more easily, lose fewer enquiries, and spend less of the week on jobs that shouldn't take one. The tools here are the simplest version of that, and they're free because they cost me very little and they genuinely help. If you ever want a hand with something bigger, I'm a text away. And if you don't, that's completely fine. Take the tools and put them to work.
